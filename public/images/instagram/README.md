# Bake O Bliss - Local Instagram Gallery Photos

To showcase your direct photos from `@bakeobliss` on the website without relying on temporary external links:

1. Save your favourite photos from your phone or Instagram camera roll into this folder:
   - `post-1.jpg` (e.g. Raspberry Rose / Vintage Cake)
   - `post-2.jpg` (e.g. Crinkle Fudge Brownies)
   - `post-3.jpg` (e.g. Floral Buttercream Cupcakes)
   - `post-4.jpg` (e.g. NYC Chunky Cookies)
   - `post-5.jpg` (e.g. Belgian Chocolate Truffle)
   - `post-6.jpg` (e.g. Tiered Celebration Cake)

2. The website will automatically display them in the Instagram Atelier section on the homepage and across the site.
3. You can also edit captions and add more posts anytime in `src/data/instagram.js`.
